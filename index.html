Frequently Asked Questions

What People Want to Know Before Working With Me

What does a business systems and growth strategist actually do?

I look at how your business gets leads, follows up, and delivers, then build the systems that make it run smoother. That means funnels, automations, CRM setup, email and content, all working together instead of in separate pieces.

I'm a coach. Do I really need GoHighLevel?

Not always. If you're juggling separate tools for your website, calendar, emails, and payments, GoHighLevel can replace most of them in one place. On our call, I'll tell you honestly whether it's right for you or whether a simpler setup will do.

I already have GoHighLevel, but it's a mess. Can you fix it?

Yes, and it's one of the most common things I do. I audit what you have, clean up broken workflows and pipelines, and rebuild what's needed so it matches how your business actually runs.

What can AI automation do for a small business?

AI automation handles the repetitive tasks that eat your time: replying to new leads, sorting inquiries, sending reminders, drafting content, and moving data between apps. You stay in control. You just stop doing the busywork.

How long does it take to build a funnel or website?

Most funnels take 1 to 3 weeks, and full websites take 2 to 6 weeks, depending on pages, copy, and integrations. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you only work with coaches?

Coaches, consultants, startups, and founders are my focus, because I understand how those businesses sell and grow. If you run a service-based business with similar needs, I can likely help too.

Can you manage my social media and content long-term?

Yes. I offer ongoing social media management, graphic design, and video editing so your brand stays consistent every week without you having to think about it.
